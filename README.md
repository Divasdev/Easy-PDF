# EasyReadPDF

> **Read for hours. Comfortably.**

EasyReadPDF is an eye-friendly, privacy-first PDF reader designed for textbooks, research papers, lecture notes, and other long-form technical documents.

Instead of changing the original PDF, EasyReadPDF creates a comfortable reading environment directly in the browser. Your PDF stays on your device — it is never uploaded to a server.

## ✨ Features

- 📄 **Client-side PDF reading** — PDFs are processed entirely in your browser.
- 🌙 **Eye-friendly reading modes** — Original, Warm, Sepia, Dark, and Soft Blue.
- ☀️ **Reading brightness control** — Adjust the perceived brightness whenever the page feels too harsh or too dim.
- ◐ **Contrast control** — Fine-tune text and page contrast independently from the selected theme.
- 🔍 **PDF search** — Find text directly inside the document.
- 🔎 **Zoom & fit controls** — Adjust the page to your preferred reading size.
- 📖 **Continuous reading** — Scroll naturally through long documents.
- ⌨️ **Keyboard shortcuts** — Navigate and control the reader without leaving the keyboard.
- 📱 **Responsive reader** — Designed to remain usable on phone-width screens.
- 🔒 **Privacy-first** — No PDF upload, no backend processing, and no account required.

## 🎨 Reading Modes

| Mode | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Original** | Read the PDF exactly as authored. |
| **Warm** | Soft cream background with comfortable dark text. |
| **Sepia** | Paper-like appearance for long reading sessions. |
| **Dark** | Dark charcoal background with soft light text. |
| **Soft Blue** | Subtle blue-gray background for a gentler reading experience. |

Every reading mode can be adjusted further with **brightness and contrast controls**, so you're not locked into a single preset.

## 🔐 Privacy

EasyReadPDF is designed around a simple principle:

> **Your PDF should stay yours.**

PDF files are processed locally in the browser. There is no server-side PDF upload or document storage.
